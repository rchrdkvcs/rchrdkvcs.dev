## Context

Inlinea is an independent studio in Rennes covering three areas: product design, websites and web applications, and custom tools and automation. The site has to present those services clearly, rank locally, and turn visitors into contact requests.

## What's inside

- A home page that walks through the three expertises, the way the studio works, and an FAQ
- One dedicated page per expertise, generated from a single data source with `[slug].astro`
- A studio page and a contact panel available from every page
- Hand-written `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` endpoints for SEO

## Stack

Astro 7, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4 and Lucide icons. Fully static, served from Cloudflare.
